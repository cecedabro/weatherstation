About the Garden Weather Station

The Station

This is a private weather station for a garden (volkstuin) near Landsmeer. Multiple ESP32 sensor nodes collect temperature, humidity, light, soil moisture, rainfall, and wind data and publish their readings over MQTT. A Raspberry Pi aggregates the data and forwards it to this backend for storage and display.
